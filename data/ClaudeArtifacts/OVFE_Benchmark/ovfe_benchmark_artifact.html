Does adding the formation energy matter?

muO is derived from each model’s own H₂ and H₂O total energies: muO = E(H₂O) − E(H₂) + ΔHf. ΔHf = 2.51 eV is the experimental (ZPE-corrected) formation enthalpy of water — the same anchor Kowalski et al. use, for the same reason (PBE-class functionals overbind O₂, so neither we nor they trust a raw O₂ total energy). Below: what every model’s Evac looks like with that term added vs. left out entirely. Because ΔHf is a constant, this shift is exactly +2.51 eV for every model — not a per-model fit — which is itself the point: without it, every single model lands far below the physically reasonable range.
